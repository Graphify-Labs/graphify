## 1. Founders, key team, and each person's research lineage

### Takeaway
World Labs was co-founded by Fei-Fei Li (CEO; ImageNet, Stanford HAI), Justin Johnson (ex-Michigan professor, ex-FAIR), Christoph Lassner (ex-Meta Reality Labs/Epic, Pulsar differentiable renderer) and Ben Mildenhall (NeRF lead author). After AMD's pending acquisition, Li becomes AMD EVP and chief scientist while Johnson and Mildenhall lead the World Labs team. The specific lineage items the requester named for Johnson (scene graphs, CLEVR, Mesh R-CNN) could not be confirmed from sources retrieved.

### Cited Findings
**Fei-Fei Li**
- Li is described as creator of ImageNet, "an Internet-scale data set containing 12 million images along with an international competition"; the first version was finished in mid-2009 (per her LinkedIn profile as summarized in search). — [Fei-Fei Li on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/fei-fei-li-4541247_imagenet-to-chatgpt-stanfords-fei-fei-li-activity-7132494938088382464-4Jx6)
- Titles reported: inaugural Sequoia Professor in Stanford's Computer Science Department; Founding Co-Director of Stanford's Human-Centered AI Institute (HAI); Director of Stanford's AI Lab 2013-2018; on sabbatical she was a Google VP and Chief Scientist of AI/ML at Google Cloud; B.A. physics Princeton, Ph.D. electrical engineering Caltech. — [AI4ALL bio](https://ai-4-all.org/our-people/fei-fei-li/); [Stanford Profiles](https://profiles.stanford.edu/fei-fei-li) (the Stanford page was reported as older and listing her only as HAI Senior Fellow, so HAI title wording differs by source)
- Memoir: "The Worlds I See: Curiosity, Exploration, and Discovery at the Dawn of AI" (published 2023), describing her path from child immigrant from China to Stanford professor. — [Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/144405196-the-worlds-i-see); [Digitopoly review](https://digitopoly.org/2024/06/27/a-review-of-fei-fei-lis-book-the-worlds-i-see/)

### Inferences
- The founding team spans the main 3D-learning lineages that led to Gaussian splatting and NeRF-style neural rendering (Mildenhall: NeRF; Lassner: differentiable sphere rendering), plus Johnson's vision-language/generative-3D background and Li's data-and-benchmark lineage. That fits World Labs choosing splats as Marble's native output (see Q5).
- Li going to AMD while Johnson and Mildenhall "continue leading the World Labs team" suggests day-to-day research and product leadership sits with the two technical co-founders.

### Gaps
- Johnson's scene graph, CLEVR and Mesh R-CNN work, and Lassner's earlier body-model/SMPL-fitting work, were not confirmed by any source I retrieved (the search that tried returned no mention of Mesh R-CNN). The report writer should not assert these without a citation.
- Lassner's current role in the AMD transaction is not stated in any retrieved source (AMD coverage names only Li, Johnson, Mildenhall).
- Other senior hires/leadership beyond the four co-founders were not found.
- Primary bio pages on worldlabs.ai were not readable.

## 2. Funding, valuation, investors, partnerships, customers and use cases

### Takeaway
World Labs left stealth in Sept 2024 with $230M at a reported ~$1B valuation, announced a $1B round on 18 Feb 2026 (Autodesk $200M; valuation reported ~$5B, ~$5.4B per Yahoo, not confirmed by the company), then agreed on 28 Sept 2026 to be acquired by AMD in an all-stock deal valued at about $8.2B, expected to close by end of 2026. Named customers are sparse: evidence is mostly vendor-published case studies and partner announcements (Autodesk, NVIDIA Isaac Sim, Rosebud AI, fal).

## 3. Timeline of products and releases (Sept 2024 to 22 Oct 2026), plus Marble pricing

### Takeaway
World Labs moved from a Sept 2024 stealth exit and a Dec 2024 single-image-to-3D preview, through a Sept 2025 Marble beta, RTFM (Oct 2025) and the public Marble launch with Chisel (12 Nov 2025), to a 2026 sequence of API (Jan), funding (Feb), Marble 1.1/1.1 Plus (Apr), Spark 2.0 (Apr), a world-model taxonomy (Jun), SceniX acquisition (Jul), Atlas omni model (Sept 1, early access) and the AMD deal (Sept 28). No release after the AMD announcement was found; no "Marble 2.0" was found.

## 4. Fei-Fei Li's essays and position statements; what World Labs is for; stated roadmap

### Takeaway
Li's core thesis (From Words to Worlds, 10 Nov 2025) is that language models are "eloquent but inexperienced, knowledgeable but ungrounded" and that spatial intelligence, delivered by generative, multimodal, physically consistent world models, is AI's next frontier, with creativity first, then robotics, then science. Subsequent publications refine this into a renderer/simulator/planner taxonomy (Jun 2026) and a hardware-closeness argument for joining AMD (Sept 2026).

### Cited Findings
- "From Words to Worlds: Spatial Intelligence is AI's Next Frontier" (about 10 Nov 2025): world models must have three capabilities, to create "with a storyteller's imagination," navigate "with a first responder's fluency," and reason about space "with scientific precision." — [Li on X thread](https://x.com/drfeifei/status/1987891813387292725); [Substack essay](https://drfeifei.substack.com/p/from-words-to-worlds-spatial-intelligence); [The Neuron summary](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/why-godmother-of-ai-dr-fei-fei-li-says-spatial-intelligence-is-ais-next-frontier/)
- Quote from the essay thread: spatial intelligence is "a technology that will turn seeing into reasoning, perception into action, and imagination into creation." — [Li on X](https://x.com/drfeifei/status/1987891210699379091?lang=en)
- Problems cited: multimodal models rarely beat chance at estimating distance, orientation and size; struggle with maze navigation and basic physics; AI-generated video loses coherence after a few seconds. — [AI syllabus summary](https://github.com/martin-martin/ai-syllabus/issues/3) (secondary summary; I did not read the essay text)
- Applications named: creativity (filmmakers, architects, game designers via Marble), robotics (machines that perceive, reason, plan and act), and science ("what if" testing in materials, biology, climate). — [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/why-godmother-of-ai-dr-fei-fei-li-says-spatial-intelligence-is-ais-next-frontier/); [Rohan Paul on X](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/1988390955620463036); [UVA Data Science summary](https://datascience.virginia.edu/news/spatial-intelligence-future-ai)
- "A Functional Taxonomy of World Models" (World Labs blog, about 3 Jun 2026): world models are sorted by function into renderers (visual fidelity; output pixels), simulators (output geometrically/physically/dynamically faithful state) and planners (given an observation and a goal, output what the agent should do next); the endpoint is "one foundation model" that renders, simulates and plans. Renderers have abundant internet video, while simulators and planners face shortages of 3D assets and robot demonstrations. — [World Labs blog](https://www.worldlabs.ai/blog/taxonomy-of-world-models); [Substack copy](https://drfeifei.substack.com/p/a-functional-taxonomy-of-world-models) (via search summary of excerpts)
- "To Seek a Newer World" (28 Sept 2026): opens with a line from Tennyson's Ulysses; calls the AMD move "a huge moment for @theworldlabs, our team, and for me"; says Lisa Su "was an early investor and believer in our mission." — [Li on X](https://x.com/drfeifei/status/2104664403681632453); [Substack](https://drfeifei.substack.com/p/worldlabs-joining-amd)
- Latent Space interview ("After LLMs: Spatial Intelligence and World Models," Li and Johnson): Marble used for generating synthetic simulation worlds for training embodied agents and robots; Johnson explains the model natively outputs splats ("tiny ... semi-transparent particle" with position and orientation). — [Latent Space](https://www.latent.space/p/after-llms-spatial-intelligence-and)
- Johnson (per a secondary summary of a Sept 2026 podcast): Marble bottlenecked all outputs through Gaussian splats, good for rendering and engine interoperability but limiting; Atlas makes new-view prediction the core primitive and generates splat worlds only when needed. Single secondary source, not checked against the original. — [My Living AI](https://www.mylivingai.com/nobody-agrees-what-a-world-model-is-justin-johnson-world-labs-explains-why/)
- Roadmap signals (stated): Atlas in early access and "coming to Marble soon"; "creative control is one of the main themes for Marble going forward" (Johnson); commitment to "widely accessible open models" post-AMD; training on AMD GPUs and hardware co-design. — [Atlas blog](https://www.worldlabs.ai/blog/atlas); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/12/fei-fei-lis-world-labs-speeds-up-the-world-model-race-with-marble-its-first-commercial-product/); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/amd-acquires-ai-legend-fei-093000475.html)

### Inferences

- Li's AMD framing (scale needs compute closeness) implies future models may be trained and served on AMD hardware first.

## 6. Competitive landscape (short)

### Takeaway
Competitors split into real-time video world models (Google Genie 3/Project Genie, Runway GWM-1, Odyssey, Decart), simulation/robotics-oriented platforms (NVIDIA Cosmos), other 3D generation (Tencent HunyuanWorld) and the JEPA-based AMI Labs. World Labs differentiates by persistent, exportable 3D assets (splats/meshes) with a creator and developer workflow, now extended toward robotics via SceniX and AMD compute.

### Cited Findings
- Google DeepMind: Genie 3 previewed Aug 2025; Project Genie opened 29 Jan 2026 to Google AI Ultra subscribers in the US; reported about 720p/24 fps (secondary). — [Google blog, Project Genie](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/project-genie/); [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/google/2026/google-deepmind-introduces-project-genie-for-interactive-ai-world-building/)
- Waymo built a variant (Waymo World Model) to simulate edge cases. — [Wikipedia, Waymo World Model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waymo_World_Model)
- AMI Labs (Yann LeCun, Paris): $1.03B seed at $3.5B pre-money; CEO Alexandre LeBrun (ex-Nabla), LeCun executive chairman; JEPA-based; co-led by Cathay Innovation, Greycroft, Hiro Capital, HV Capital and Bezos Expeditions; strategic investors reportedly include Nvidia, Toyota, Samsung, Temasek; CEO says at least a year of research needed before first application. — [TechCrunch, 9 Mar 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/09/yann-lecuns-ami-labs-raises-1-03-billion-to-build-world-models/); [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/yann-lecun-ami-labs-world-models-billion); [Futurum](https://futurumgroup.com/insights/yann-lecuns-ami-raises-1bn-seed-round-is-the-world-model-era-finally-here/)

- Corporate alignment: World Labs now has AMD (and earlier investor NVIDIA); Google, NVIDIA (Cosmos), Tencent and AMI Labs are building competing stacks with their own compute or capital.
